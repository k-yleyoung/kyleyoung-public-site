Email kyle@kyleyoung.dev with “Pintrail” in the subject.

Include your iPhone model, iOS and app version, and the steps that led to the issue. Avoid sending private videos, location details, passwords, or payment information unless you deliberately choose to share a relevant sample.

Finding a flight

Use a steady camera with a clearly visible ball and space above it. Set impact near contact, choose a flight window, then tap Find ball flight. Review a candidate over the video and keep it only if it follows your ball. Motion detection may find another moving object or return no result. Use the manual launch, flight, and finish anchors when needed.

A tracer is an image path. It does not provide measured carry, speed, spin, or a geographic ball position. Mark origins and landings yourself on the map; course-search pins are course locations, not surveyed flags.

Saving and sharing

Save & close keeps the editable clip in your journal. Export traced video creates a separate MP4. Use Save to Photos or Share MP4 to keep that movie outside the app; temporary export files can be cleaned up. Pintrail exports omit source GPS metadata, but the picture and audio can still reveal people or places.

Plus and restoring purchases

Pintrail Plus removes ads with a non-consumable lifetime purchase. The app shows your localized App Store price before confirmation. It has no renewal. Open Plus → Restore purchases while using the Apple Account that made the purchase. If access still does not appear, check your connection and contact support. Apple handles payment and refund requests.

Development builds labeled “Purchase testing · no real money charged” use RevenueCat Test Store. These test entitlements are separate from App Store purchases. Pintrail is currently in development and is not available for public download.

Your local journal

The current app has no Pintrail cloud sync. Uninstalling removes its local journal. Keep important video exports in Photos or another destination you control. Device backups are managed separately by iOS and your settings.
