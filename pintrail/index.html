YOUR GAME, SHOT BY SHOT

Keep the round.
See the flight.

Your golf videos, scorecard, and mapped shots, together in one private iPhone journal.

Pintrail is in development. It is not currently available for public download. This site provides support and privacy information for development and future App Store builds.

Give it a little flight.

Record or import a shot, look for a visible flight on device, review the path, and export a traced video. Manual anchors are there when you need them.

Remember the whole round.

Keep nine- or eighteen-hole scores, putts, clubs, and golfer-marked shot origins and landings. Revisit the moments in your journal.

Core local tools are free. Pintrail Plus removes ads with a one-time lifetime purchase when available through the App Store. The app displays the price for your region before purchase.

Tracers show a reviewed two-dimensional image path. Pintrail does not measure carry, speed, spin, or a geographic landing from video. The golfer confirms the ball’s position on the map.
