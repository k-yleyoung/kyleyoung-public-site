Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026. Applies to Crowd Track 1.2 and later.

Crowd Track shows how busy the places around you are. This page says what the app collects to do that, what it does not collect, and how to remove it. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking across other apps or websites.

1. Your account

There is no sign-up form. The app creates an account for your phone the first time you open it. Accounts from versions before 1.2 are not carried over: everyone starts with a new username.

  • A random account id and a generated username, which you can change.
  • Whether to count you as a guy or a girl, for each place's mix. You choose this.
  • A profile photo, if you add one.
  • A sign-in token and an install key kept in your phone's Keychain. Our server stores only a one-way hash of each.
  • When your account was last active.

2. Location

Location is optional. Without it you can still browse.

  • Counting you in. Your phone works out by itself whether you are at one of the listed places. When you arrive it tells our server which place, and when you leave it says so. Your position is not sent for this. With location set to Always this also works while the app is closed; with While Using it works only while the app is open.
  • What is near you. When the app loads the places and posts around you, it sends your position so the server can pick them. The server does not keep that position. It keeps only a rounded one, accurate to about a kilometre (about half a mile), updated at most every ten minutes, to decide which deal notifications are relevant to you.
  • Posts. A post is stored with a position rounded the same way, so it can be shown to people nearby. Never your exact position.
  • Crowd and cover reports. Your position is checked once, to confirm you are at the place you are reporting on, and is not stored.

While you are at a place you are one more in its count. Other people see the number, never who is there.

When a stay ends we keep a record of it for forecasting: the place, guy or girl, when it started and when it ended. That record is not linked to your account.

3. What you write and share

  • Posts and comments, with any photo you attach, and your votes. Posts are deleted after seven days.
  • Direct messages and their photos, until you or the other person delete the conversation.
  • Photos are re-encoded on upload, which removes embedded information such as where they were taken.
  • Crowd levels, cover charges and "going tonight" answers you submit.

Anonymous posts. When you post or comment anonymously, other people are not shown your username or photo, and the app is built so that other people cannot connect your anonymous posts to your name. Someone who blocks an anonymous poster stops seeing everything that poster writes anonymously, and can tell from that that one person wrote it, but not who. Our server does know which account wrote each post, so that blocking, reporting and bans work.

4. Notifications

If you allow notifications we store the push token Apple gives your phone, and send notifications through Apple's Push Notification service. You can turn each kind off in the app.

5. Moderation

  • If you report something, we keep the report and a copy of what was reported.
  • If you block someone, we keep the block so it stays in force.
  • We may remove content or ban accounts that break the rules. A ban record includes its reason and how long it lasts.

6. Where it is kept and who else sees it

  • Crowd Track runs on a server we operate ourselves. Traffic reaches it through Cloudflare, which protects it and necessarily handles the requests in transit.
  • Apple delivers notifications, as described above.
  • We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers, and the app contains no third-party analytics or advertising code.
  • We may disclose information if the law requires it.

7. How long it is kept

  • Posts: seven days. Activity notifications: sixty days.
  • A check-in: until you leave, or a few hours at most if your phone never reports the exit.
  • Sign-in tokens that have not been used for about thirteen months are removed.
  • Counts per place per hour, and the records of stays described above, are kept. They do not identify anyone.
  • Everything else: until you delete it or your account.

8. Deleting your data

In the app, open your profile and choose Delete my data. This removes your account, username, photo, posts, comments, votes, conversations and messages from our server straight away. You can also email us and we will do it for you.

9. Age

Crowd Track is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date at the top.
