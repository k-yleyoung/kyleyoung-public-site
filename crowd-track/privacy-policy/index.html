Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2025

1. Information We Collect

Device Information:

  • Your device's unique identifier (UUID) to create and manage your account.
  • This identifier is generated by iOS.

Chat Data:

  • Messages you send through the chat feature.
  • Records of user bans and their durations.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our service.
  • To moderate chat content and enforce community guidelines.
  • To identify and prevent abuse.
  • To improve user experience and app functionality.

3. Data Storage and Security

  • Chat messages are stored securely on our servers.
  • User identifiers are stored to manage chat access and moderation.
  • We implement appropriate security measures to protect your data.

4. User Bans and Moderation

  • We reserve the right to ban users who violate our terms of service.
  • Banned users will be notified of their ban status and unban date.
  • Ban decisions are made at our sole discretion.

5. Data Retention

  • Chat messages are retained as necessary to provide our services.
  • You may request deletion of your data by contacting us.

6. Third-Party Services

  • We use Firebase for data storage and authentication.
  • See Google's privacy policy for how they handle data.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
