An iOS app by Kyle Young

Make the moment yours.

PersonaClips is being built to turn original ideas and authorized portraits into short AI-generated videos.

In development. PersonaClips is not yet available for public download or purchase.

Built around creative permission

The app is designed for adults to create original scenes from text or their own portrait. Friend portraits require a direct consent pass from the person depicted. Generated media is private in the app, and exported videos are labeled as AI-generated.

PersonaClips is not intended for impersonation, celebrity likenesses, or unlicensed movie, music, and meme content.
