PersonaClips support

For app help, privacy requests, or concerns about content and likeness consent, email:

kyle@kyleyoung.dev

In development. PersonaClips is currently being tested and has not launched publicly.

App or credit issues

Describe what happened and include your app version, approximate time, and any relevant generation or transaction reference available in the app. For an Apple billing or refund request, use Apple’s Report a Problem.

Please do not email passwords, API keys, payment card information, or private photos.

Privacy and account requests

Use “PersonaClips privacy” in the email subject and describe your request. Account deletion is available through the app’s account settings. If you cannot access your account, contact the address above for help.

Likeness, consent, and content concerns

Use “PersonaClips content concern” in the subject. Describe the issue and include a generation reference or the location of the material, if available. A person who has issued a friend consent pass can revoke it in the app.

Avoid attaching sensitive media. If more information is needed to review the concern, we can discuss an appropriate way to provide it.
